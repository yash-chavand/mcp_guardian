# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and respectful to people of all backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the work.
- Share security issues privately and avoid publishing exploit details before a fix is available.
- Respect privacy; do not post credentials, personal data, or private traffic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation. Reports will be handled with care and confidentiality.
